# How to Download and Install ReelRadar on Your Mobile Phone 📱
*(No coding knowledge required!)*

Follow this simple 3-step guide to get **ReelRadar** running on your Android phone.

---

## Step 1: Get the APK File

You have two super easy ways to generate your APK file:

### Method A: Automated Cloud Build (Easiest - 100% Free on GitHub)
If you don't want to install any developer software on your computer:
1. Open [GitHub.com](https://github.com/) and create a free account (if you don't already have one).
2. Create a new repository called `ReelRadar`.
3. Upload the files from this folder (`C:\Users\mt089\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\ReelRadar`) to your repository.
4. Click on the **Actions** tab at the top of your GitHub repository.
5. You will see a workflow called **"Build ReelRadar APK"** running automatically!
6. In about 2 minutes, click on the completed run and download the **`ReelRadar-Debug-APK`** directly to your phone or computer.

---

### Method B: 1-Click Builder on Your Computer
If you have **Android Studio** or **Java** installed on your PC:
1. Simply double-click the file named **`build-apk.bat`** in this folder.
2. The script will automatically compile everything.
3. When finished, it will automatically produce **`ReelRadar-v1.0.apk`** and open the folder for you!

---

## Step 2: Transfer and Install the APK on Your Phone

1. **Send the APK to your phone**:
   - Send `ReelRadar-v1.0.apk` to yourself via **Google Drive**, **WhatsApp**, or plug your phone into your PC via **USB**.
2. **Tap the APK file on your phone**:
   - Your phone will ask: *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*.
   - Tap **Settings** and toggle **"Allow from this source"** (this is standard for any app downloaded outside the Google Play Store).
3. Tap **Install** and wait 5 seconds.
4. Tap **Open**! 🎉

---

## Step 3: First-Time Setup on Your Phone

When you open ReelRadar for the first time:

1. **Onboarding Screen**:
   - Tap **"Enable Accessibility Detector"**: Your phone will open the Accessibility menu. Tap **Downloaded Apps** > **ReelRadar Scroll Detector** > Toggle **ON**.
   - Tap **"Enable Floating Overlay"**: Toggle ON **Allow display over other apps**.
2. **Start Tracking**:
   - Open **Instagram** and switch to the **Reels** tab!
   - You will see a discreet floating counter in the corner showing `0 / 50`.
   - Scroll through reels naturally — ReelRadar will keep you mindful, show your daily progress ring, and prompt you to take a breath when you reach your limit!

---

## Need Help?
- **Service turns off by itself?** Go to your phone's *Settings > Apps > ReelRadar > Battery* and select **"Unrestricted"** so your phone's power saver doesn't pause the background counter.
- **Want to change your daily limit?** Open ReelRadar > tap the slider icon (**Limits**) > adjust your daily target from 10 to 200 reels!
